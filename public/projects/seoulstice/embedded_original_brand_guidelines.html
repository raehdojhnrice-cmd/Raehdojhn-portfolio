i.EssenceChapter One

A moment between
waking and becoming.

Seoulstice is the hush before the city wakes — the soft breath where Seoul's modernity meets a solstice calm.

A portmanteau of Seoul and Solstice. The brand exists at the intersection of hypermodern Korean ritual and the cosmic alignment of renewal — a daily ceremony pressed into the body of a single morning.

Where K-beauty futurism meets old-money calm. Where 90s Seoul street poetry meets the Tumblr-era intimacy of soft, dewy beauty. Where ritual is not a marketing word but a discipline practiced before sunrise, in pastel-tiled bathrooms across continents.

This is the brand director log — a bible for the visual, verbal, and textural language of Seoulstice. Use it as scripture, not suggestion.

iv.The MarkChapter Four

A wordmark, a ritual.

The mark must always breathe.

The Seoulstice wordmark is a calligraphic gesture — never compressed, never warped, never coloured outside the palette. Set in DM Serif Display Italic, with stice rendered in burgundy as the only chromatic break.

The Korean reading "설스티스" appears beneath the wordmark. Always together. Never apart.

  • Min. Size 32px digital
  • Clear Space 1x cap height
  • On Image 15-30% opacity
  • Light Mode Espresso on beige
  • Dark Mode Ivory on espresso-deep
  • Accent Burgundy italic only

viii.PackagingChapter Eight

Objects of quiet ritual.

Every vessel carries the ceremony. Matte ceramic finishes, soft-touch plastics, uncoated cardstock — each material chosen because it wants to be touched. The logo sits in espresso ink, always in Old Money serif, always accompanied by its Korean counterpart.

Pollock splashes appear as whispered accents — printed thin-filament gestures, never louder than the product itself.

Material standards

Every surface must want to be held. Ceramic finish for jars, soft-touch for tubes, frosted glass for pump bottles, uncoated fibrous cardstock for boxes. No gloss. No foil. No lamination.

  • Tube surface Soft-touch matte
  • Pump body Frosted glass / ceramic
  • Jar finish Matte ceramic glaze
  • Box stock Uncoated 300gsm
  • Ink system Espresso + burgundy only
  • Cap finish Brushed aluminium

Branding on packaging

The wordmark runs vertically on tubes and centered on pump bottles and jars. Korean characters always present. Pollock-style thin-filament gestures may appear as a single printed stroke behind the logotype — never more than one per surface.

  • Logo placement Centered or vertical
  • Korean mark Always present
  • Pollock accents 1 per surface max
  • Microtext SST codes on back panel
  • Color on product Palette tones only
  • Dieline system Swiss-grid aligned
