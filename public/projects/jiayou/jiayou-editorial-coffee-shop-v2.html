03 · Embedded Brand Guidelines

System, not decoration.

An interactive guideline section for your portfolio: palette, typography, logo logic, micrographics, microtext placements, and photo direction in one embedded HTML block.

Color logic

Muted caffeine. Soft emotion. Small gold hits.

The palette should feel like ceramic glaze, steamed milk, receipt paper, and a jade parasol under daylight. Color supports calm rather than shouting for attention.

Typography
Fuel for the dreamers.

Condensed hero type for editorial signage, menu boards, and social covers.

加油。

Noto Serif JP / Mincho-style cultural accent. Used like a whisper, not a logo replacement.

Helvetica Neue / Inter / Avenir Next for body copy, product cards, receipts, UI labels, and calm retail clarity.

Logo + micrographics

Cat spiral, JY roundel, lucky cloud, fuel gauge, mountain mist, seal chop.

Motifs should sit on edges, cup sleeves, receipt corners, sticker sheets, or café walls. Keep opacity under 80% and avoid overcrowding the product moment.
