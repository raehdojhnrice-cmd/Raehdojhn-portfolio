## Psychedelic Poster Frame (Expanded Editorial Prompt)  
**Prompt:** A **collaged 1960s LSD-poster-inspired fashion editorial still**, restaged with hyperreal tactility. The image fractures between photography and graphic hallucination. A **blurred model’s head tilts backward in ecstatic surrender**, eyes half-closed, mouth parted as if caught between ecstasy and collapse. Their face is **duplicated into overlapping silhouettes**, each layer sliding like ghost echoes — inverted outlines drifting apart, creating a spectral afterimage of identity.  
The model’s limbs appear tangled, bodies half-nude, elongated, stretched across the poster as if submerged underwater. Gestures blur into abstraction, limbs dissolving into light trails, as though rebellion itself is liquefied into ritual performance.  
**Typography sprawls diagonally across the frame** in warped serif lettering, fractured and psychedelic. Acid-yellow slogans slash against deep indigo-blue monochrome, letters bleeding into limbs, warped like melting vinyl. Text does not sit on top of the composition — it fuses into the skin, becoming part of the hallucination.  
  
**Angles & Framing:**

* **Tilted Diagonal Crop:** Limbs tangle and overlap text diagonals, torsos intersect with slogans — editorial chaos designed like a concert flyer.   
* **Overhead Fragment:** Viewed from above, limbs dissolve into posterized shadows, like dancers blurred into smoke and ink. The abstraction feels archival, like a forensic poster scan rediscovered.   
  
**Lighting & Palette:**

**Textures & Editorial Noise:**

**Mood:** Rebellion staged as hallucination. **Luxury refracted through counterculture protest.** Fashion dissolves into manifesto — an LSD ritual printed into permanence. The image vibrates between rebellion, nostalgia, and sacred spectacle.
