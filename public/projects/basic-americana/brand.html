01 / Positioning

The thesis

Promise plain clothes for emotional weather

Ordinary LA basics, priced like basics, styled like fake heritage. The store stays clean and shoppable; the culture around it gets loud — stickers, receipts, profile cards, and a strip-mall scroll world.

07 / Usage

Rules

Do

  • Keep the store grid clean and shoppable
  • Let stickers live in controlled modules
  • Use real product photography
  • Track microtext tight and uppercase

Don't

  • Copy real brand logos or mastheads
  • Fake metrics, reviews, or influencers
  • Let motion block reading
  • Over-clutter the product cards
