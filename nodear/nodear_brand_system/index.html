CONTRIBUTION FILE — OPEN BRIEF / ND-COL-001 / FILED: 2026

This is not a job posting. This is not a creative brief for hire. This is a file opened inside the NODEAR archive. If this document has reached you, the file is already open.

Three types of contribution.
You are one of them.

TYPE 01

SOUND

You contribute an element — drums, a bass sequence, a texture, a field recording that carries weight. Not a finished track. An element the archive processes. What you make will be heard in a context you didn't design, and that context will be more than either of you could have made alone.

TYPE 02

VISUAL

You make images. Photography, design, illustration, motion. You understand the difference between a moodboard and an operating system. You can read the visual DNA codex and immediately know whether your work fits without being told.

TYPE 03

OBJECT

You make things that exist in the physical world. Packaging, print, apparel, hardware. You know what a numbered edition means — not as a marketing tactic, but as a decision about scarcity and meaning.

The Terms

  • Your contribution is filed with your name, date, and contribution type in the NODEAR archive.
  • Your work is credited in all public releases where it appears.
  • You receive a physical Archive Edition artifact from the run you contributed to — numbered, filed, yours.
  • You are listed as a contributor. Someone who understood the brief and added to the record. Not a vendor. Not a featured artist.

"The system elevates the contribution. The contributor becomes part of the record. The record outlasts both."
